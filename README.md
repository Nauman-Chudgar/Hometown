# 🌍 Discover Modasa – A City of Heritage, Nature & Memories

Modasa Tourism Guide is a tourism-focused project that showcases the beauty, attractions, hospitality, and cultural charm of Modasa, Gujarat, India. It aims to help visitors discover interesting places, plan outings, and explore the city through beautiful visuals and useful travel information.

## 📌 About the Project

Modasa, located in Gujarat's Aravalli district, is a city with a blend of local culture, religious places, natural surroundings, and modern amenities.

This project presents a curated guide to popular tourist attractions, recommended hotels, memorable quotes, and creative visuals celebrating the city.

## ✨ Features

- 🏞️ Tourist Attractions: Discover popular places in and around Modasa.
- 🏨 Hotel Recommendations: Explore selected hotels for a comfortable stay.
- 📸 Visual Gallery: Enjoy scenic city-inspired images and tourist destination photos.
- 💬 City Slogans & Quotes: Celebrate the memories and identity of Modasa.
- 🗺️ Travel Inspiration: Help visitors identify places for sightseeing, family outings, and trips with friends.
- ❤️ Local Pride: Highlight the special connection people have with their hometown.

## 📍 Top Tourist Attractions

1. Odhari Lake – A place to enjoy peaceful surroundings and evening views.
2. Aqualand Water Park – A fun destination for friends and families.
3. Shamlaji Vishnu Temple – A famous religious and architectural attraction near Modasa.
4. Zanzari Waterfall – A scenic destination for nature lovers.
5. Polo Forest – A destination known for greenery, historic ruins, and outdoor exploration.

Note: Some attractions are located outside Modasa city. Check routes, access, and seasonal conditions before visiting.
