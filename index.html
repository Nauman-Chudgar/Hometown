Visit Modasa

Where Nature Smiles and Culture Shines! A City of Heritage, Heart, and Endless Memories.

Top 5 Tourist Attractions

1. Odhari Lake

A peaceful lake area where you can enjoy some fresh air

2. Aqualand Water Park

Enjoy water rides and a fun day out

3. Shamlaji Vishnu Temple

A famous temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu. One of the best destinations for a family trip from Modasa.

4. Zanzari Waterfall

The waterfall and surrounding landscape are especially attractive during the rainy season

5. Polo Forest

A great choice for a longer trip with friends. The forest is particularly attractive during and after the monsoon.
